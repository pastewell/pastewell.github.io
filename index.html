Pastewell

A fast, private clipboard manager for Mac, iPhone, and iPad.

In development. Coming to the Mac and the App Store.

Keyboard first

Press ⇧⌘V, type a few letters, press Return. The list opens right at your text cursor, and every shortcut can be changed. Long entries show how many more lines they hold, and hovering shows a preview and when you copied it.

One paste, many items

Select several items and paste them together in a single step, text, images, and files alike. One undo takes it all back.

Clean plain text

Press ⌃Return in the list, or ⌃V anywhere, to paste clean plain text: no formatting, smart quotes, or invisible characters, with accents and emoji kept. ⌘V always pastes your latest copy unchanged.

Copy text from the screen

Press ⇧⌘6 and drag a box anywhere on the screen, even over a picture. Pastewell reads the text in it on your Mac and copies it. macOS asks for Screen Recording permission the first time.

Mark up screenshots

On the Mac, add arrows, text, boxes, and spotlights to any image in your history, or redact part of it. Redaction removes the pixels underneath, not just covers them. Save & Copy makes a new item and leaves the original as it was.

Favorites as snippets

Star anything you reuse and it becomes a Favorite, kept until you unstar it. Give Favorites a name and edit their text, and they work as reusable snippets. On the Mac, drag them into any order you like.

iPhone and iPad too

Your Favorites and recent copies, wherever you are. Tap to copy, or copy as clean plain text. Save what is on the clipboard with the Paste button, and touch and hold an item to preview it and see when it was copied. Everything syncs through your own private iCloud. Pastewell has no servers and no accounts.

Private by design

No analytics, no tracking, no ads. Password managers and concealed copies are never recorded.

Free, for good

No subscriptions and nothing locked away. Tips are welcome but never required. It started from Maccy, the much-loved open-source clipboard manager, and the source will be public so you can check exactly what it does.

Privacy

Your clipboard history is stored on your devices. When it syncs, it travels only through your own private iCloud account, and you can turn sync off. Copied content is stored encrypted in your iCloud; larger items such as images are stored as iCloud files, which are end-to-end encrypted only when you turn on Advanced Data Protection. Text you copy from the screen is recognized on your Mac, not online. Pastewell sends nothing anywhere else, apart from checking for updates in the Mac version, which you can switch off. If you choose to leave a tip, payment is handled by Apple or by the tip page in your browser.
